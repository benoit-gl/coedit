These are small in-process observations with only three samples and substantial warm-up/order variation. They do not establish a performance guarantee or growth bound. The one-marker-per-edit custom shape is much simpler than the production command graph and does not store equivalent attribution semantics to native text; cross-layout timings measure these explicitly different storage mechanics. No event waiting, UI, network, Automerge sync, batch serialization, derived-index replay, final encoding, or admission validation is inside timed intervals. Native receiver apply includes native application only; separate checks occur after episode boundaries. Framing is modeled as 4 bytes per packet plus 4 bytes per change, without compression.
